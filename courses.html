Track One

AI & Security

Navigate the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity from practical use cases to governance and risk awareness for modern organisations.

AI in modern security operations

This track explores how AI is shaping cybersecurity practice, operational workflows, risk management, and the broader technology landscape.

AI fundamentals for security professionals Core concepts, terminology, adoption patterns, and where AI fits within current operating environments.
Threat detection and operational use How AI can support analysis, automation, prioritisation, and security decision-making.
AI governance and risk Bias, oversight, data exposure, policy needs, and governance considerations for responsible adoption.
Practical implementation considerations Readiness, controls, stakeholder alignment, and integrating AI into existing processes responsibly.
AI Awareness Security Context Governance

Track Two

Networking & Collaboration

Build stronger foundational and applied understanding across enterprise networking, secure connectivity, collaboration platforms, and communication environments.

Connected infrastructure for modern teams

This track covers the principles and realities of modern networking and collaboration environments, with practical relevance for both technical and cross-functional audiences.

Networking fundamentals Core networking concepts, architecture, traffic flow, segmentation, resilience, and secure design thinking.
Enterprise networking context Infrastructure patterns, operational dependencies, performance considerations, and modern enterprise expectations.
Collaboration platforms Digital workplace tools, platform strategy, experience design, and adoption considerations for modern teams.
Voice and video environments Unified communications, communication quality, user experience, and the operational role of voice and video systems.
Networking Collaboration Voice & Video

Best suited for

Security professionals People who need stronger infrastructure understanding to improve security judgement.
Operations and workplace teams Stakeholders involved in communications capability, user experience, and service delivery.
Track Three

Security & Governance

Develop stronger understanding of governance, policy, risk, and the practical foundations required to manage cybersecurity in a structured and business-aligned way.

Foundations for resilient security programs

This track focuses on the governance and management disciplines that underpin effective security programs useful for both dedicated security roles and broader organisational leaders.

Governance and accountability Roles, decision structures, oversight models, and leadership engagement in cybersecurity.
Risk and control thinking Risk assessment, prioritisation, treatment planning, and practical security management approaches.
Policy and operating structure How policy, standards, procedures, and training work together in a mature environment.
NIST CSF and GDPR context Understanding how recognised frameworks and obligations shape governance and operational security expectations.
Governance Risk Policy

Best suited for

Security managers Leaders responsible for shaping capability, accountability, and programme direction.
Governance & compliance teams Professionals who need stronger practical grounding in security structure and control thinking.
Executives and operational leads Decision-makers who need clearer visibility into cyber risk, responsibility, and governance practice.

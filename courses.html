Our Story

Experience that connects security, technology and business.

Founded by cybersecurity professional and collaboration expert Joshua Alero, AleroSec brings over a decade of experience helping organisations protect enterprise environments, reduce cyber risk, and strengthen digital resilience. With deep expertise in cybersecurity, enterprise collaboration platforms, and complex IT infrastructures, Joshua has designed, deployed, supported, and troubleshot large-scale enterprise systems and business-critical applications across demanding environments. Through AleroSec, he helps organisations navigate modern security challenges by delivering cybersecurity advisory services, risk assessments, compliance guidance, business continuity strategies, AI security frameworks, and expert-led IT enablement and training programmes. Combining technical depth with a business-focused approach, Joshua empowers organisations to build secure, resilient, and future-ready technology environments.

Every engagement is grounded in practical experience: understanding the technology landscape, clarifying the business context, and helping teams turn security priorities into sustainable action.
